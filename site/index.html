01 · THE PARADIGM COMPARISON

From hand-coded variation
to semantic evolution

LLM-ES combines the persistent state and selection pressure of evolutionary search with the semantic generation capabilities of foundation models.

SHORT-HORIZON GENERATION

Independent sampling

Ordinary repeated generation can produce diverse answers, but usually lacks persistent population state and evolutionary pressure.

  • Limited cross-iteration memory
  • No explicit archive or genealogy
  • Weak long-horizon search control
CLASSICAL SEARCH
Evolutionary computation

Hand-designed operators

Vectors, discrete encodings, and symbolic trees are evolved through fixed mutation and crossover mechanisms.

  • Low-level search representations
  • Explicit operator design
  • Fitness-driven selection
SEMANTIC SEARCH
LLM-guided evolutionary search

Prompt-conditioned variation

Language models rewrite, patch, critique, and fuse structured artifacts under iterative evaluator feedback.

  • Code, algorithms, prompts, and workflows
  • Population and archive memory
  • Long-horizon verified improvement
